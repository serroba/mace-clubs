Rotation plane

Each dot is one raw gyroscope sample during a work set, projected onto a plane (deg/s on each axis). A tight ring or elongated blob off-center from zero shows a consistent, dominant rotation plane; a diffuse scatter near the origin shows little rotation in that plane.
