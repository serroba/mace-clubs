# Mace and Clubs v0.9.0: the bulava joins the rack

A third implement joins the mace and the clubs: the **bulava**, the single
heavy club. It has its own default weight (6 kg, editable on the watch and
in Garmin Connect), its own movement vocabulary — the **mill**, the
**reverse mill**, and the **bullwhip** — and its own smoothness history, so
scores are never compared across implements.

The headline is the **Combo**, the traditional bulava combination set:
mill, reverse mill, and bullwhip on one hand, then the same sequence
mirrored on the other. Pick it when starting a bulava workout and the
metronome calls the sequence for you — an accented pulse on every movement
change and a distinct double pulse on the hand switch, so you never need to
look down mid-swing. When you do glance, the screen shows the current hand
and movement (`L REV MILL`) instead of the tempo.

Beats per movement are configurable from Garmin Connect (mill, reverse
mill, and bullwhip each get their own count; the default is 4-4-2). Combo
sets are recorded with an alternating working side, since one full cycle
works both hands equally.

## Included in this release

- Bulava as a dedicated implement with its own weight and history
- Mill, reverse mill, and bullwhip movements for the bulava
- The Combo: the traditional sequence with mirrored hands
- Metronome sequence calling: movement-change accents and a hand-switch
  double pulse
- Current hand and movement on screen during combo work
- Per-movement combo beats configurable from the phone (default 4-4-2)
