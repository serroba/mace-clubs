# Mace & Clubs v0.17.0: watches with no MENU button can now open settings and, during rest, change movement or side - tap the hint at the bottom of the screen

34 changes since v0.16.0 (2026-09-08).

## On the watch

- Watches with no MENU button can now open settings and, during rest, change movement or side - tap the hint at the bottom of the screen. Fixes a paused workout being saved when the screen said it would be discarded. ([#185](https://github.com/serroba/mace-clubs/pull/185))
- On the Instinct Crossover the watch hands move aside while the workout summary is up, so the numbers underneath stay readable. ([#181](https://github.com/serroba/mace-clubs/pull/181))
- The app now starts on the Descent G1 and Instinct Crossover too, which share that memory limit and the same reduced build. ([#174](https://github.com/serroba/mace-clubs/pull/174))
- The app now starts on Instinct 2, 2S and 2X. It ran out of memory on launch before, and those watches run a reduced build to fit: no history browser, on-watch workout editor or Connect motion charts. ([#172](https://github.com/serroba/mace-clubs/pull/172))
- Work intervals now show in colour on watches with colour displays. ([#166](https://github.com/serroba/mace-clubs/pull/166))
- Garmin Connect now charts each detected swing, not just the running total. ([#163](https://github.com/serroba/mace-clubs/pull/163))
- Fixed the swing cadence chart, which reported impossible values. ([#162](https://github.com/serroba/mace-clubs/pull/162))
- Smoothness is now called the Rhythm Score. ([#156](https://github.com/serroba/mace-clubs/pull/156))
- The app is now called Mace & Clubs everywhere it can be. ([#152](https://github.com/serroba/mace-clubs/pull/152))
