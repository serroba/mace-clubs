# Mace and Clubs v0.8.0: train like the akhara

Mace and clubs now train differently, the way their traditions do.

Starting a workout asks for your implement and then your movement: the mace
offers the **360**, the **10-to-2**, and free flow, while clubs offer the
**mill**, the **shield cast**, and free flow. Your choice appears on the
GET READY screen, is remembered as your default, and is saved with every
work block in the activity.

During a free-training rest, hold **MENU** to switch the movement or the
working side for your next set without leaving the session. Single-side sets
are tallied per hand and the workout summary shows an **L/R balance tag** —
traditional gada training gives each hand the same number of sets, and now
the watch keeps score.

Two **Challenge** presets bring the akhara competition format: 5 or 10
minutes of continuous swinging, no rest. Challenges turn on the new
accelerometer **swing counter**, which replaces metronome rounds on screen
with your detected swing count and writes per-set and total swings into the
activity. You can also enable the counter for regular sessions from Settings
(it uses more battery).

## Included in this release

- Movement choice in the start flow, scoped to mace or club movements
- Movement and working-side switching from the free-training rest menu
- Per-hand set tally with an L/R balance tag in the workout summary
- Challenge 5:00 and 10:00 presets with forced swing counting
- Accelerometer swing counter with per-lap and session FIT fields
- Free-training screen layout fixes around the Instinct subwindow
