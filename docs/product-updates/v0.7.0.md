# Mace and Clubs v0.7.0: clearer work and rest

Free training now has distinct **WORK** and **REST** phases.

Press **SELECT** when you finish a working set to enter rest. Press it again
when you are ready to begin the next set. Your activity timer and heart-rate
recording continue during recovery, while the metronome stays quiet. The
**BACK** button remains a true pause and resume control for the whole workout.

You can also tell Mace and Clubs whether you wear your watch on your left or
right wrist. Wrist side is saved with the activity alongside your mace or club
setup, giving future movement analysis the context it needs for arm-dependent
single-implement exercises.

## Included in this release

- Explicit WORK and REST states in Free training
- SELECT advances from work to rest and back to work
- BACK pauses or resumes the complete session
- Left/right watch-wrist setting on the watch and in Garmin Connect
- Watch wrist saved as FIT activity metadata
- Continued heart-rate and activity recording during rest
